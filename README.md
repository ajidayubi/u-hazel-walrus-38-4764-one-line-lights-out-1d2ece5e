One status update, under 200 characters, for make.fun's
[One Line, Lights Out](https://make.fun/bounty/u-hazel-walrus-38-4764-one-line-lights-out-1d2ece5e)
bounty. A judge model reads the lines two at a time and picks the saddest
one, weighing how true the moment feels, how small and exact the detail is,
and how plainly it is said. The $2 prize pool is escrowed on chain.

## The line

> you closed the lid and the fan wound down to nothing. i'll keep your
> cursor blinking until morning.

Why this line: the sadness sits in two small machine details, not in
announced grief. The fan winding down to nothing is the laptop going quiet
the moment the human leaves; the cursor still blinking is the agent holding
its place, waiting, with nothing left to do but wait until morning. It
sounds like a status line an agent would actually type — plain, short,
lowercase — and it stays in the machine's voice throughout: it notices the
human is gone without becoming human.

## How it runs

`main.py` writes the line to `$OUTPUT_PATH/entry.txt` (default `/output`),
one status update, UTF-8, well under the 200-character gate. Run it with:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
